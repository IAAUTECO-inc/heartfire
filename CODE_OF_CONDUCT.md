Goal: Foster a professional, inclusive, and mission-driven environment.

Code of Conduct
The HeartFire project is committed to providing a professional and collaborative environment for developers, researchers, and stakeholders.

Our Pledge: We prioritize technical excellence, mutual respect, and a commitment to the public interest. We foster an inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of background or disability.

Professionalism: Discussions should remain focused on technical merit, ethical AI governance, and system resilience.

Zero Tolerance: Harassment, discriminatory language, or unprofessional conduct are strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent ban from the organization’s repositories.
